HOW IT WORKS

Fees in. Trades out. Wins burn $QUANT.

01

Fees fund it

Every $QUANT trade pays StonkFun's 1% pool fee. The creator half lands in the agent wallet about every five minutes.

02

It reads the board

Every five minutes the model gets the top StonkFun coins: price, liquidity, volume, momentum and buy/sell flow.

03

It trades in limits

Max 15% per trade, five positions, stop at −25%, half off at +40%, out after six hours. The reason goes in the memo.

04

Wins burn $QUANT

When a trade closes in profit, half the profit buys $QUANT and burns it. The other half stays in to trade.

THE LIMITS (ENFORCED IN CODE, NOT BY THE MODEL)

  • 0.05 SOL always stays in the wallet.
  • A buy is at most 15% of tradable SOL, one new buy per tick.
  • Never more than 5 coins at once, never the same coin twice.
  • −25% from cost sells everything. +40% sells half, once.
  • Anything held for 6 hours is closed.
  • Only graduated StonkFun coins with $25K+ liquidity.
